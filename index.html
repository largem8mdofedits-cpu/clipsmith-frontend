Questions

Good to know.

What happens after my free hour runs out?

You'll be prompted to pick a plan before any further renders start that week — nothing renders or charges automatically. Your free hour refills again at the start of your next weekly cycle, for as long as you stay on the free plan.

Do exports have a watermark?

No. Every plan, including your weekly free hour, exports in full quality with no watermark.

Can I cancel or switch plans anytime?

Yes — upgrade, downgrade, or cancel from your billing settings whenever you like. Changes apply from your next billing cycle.

What's the campaign marketplace?

A feed of active paid clipping campaigns with source footage already attached, so you can start cutting and submitting without hunting for gigs elsewhere.
